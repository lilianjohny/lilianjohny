# viral-hooks.md — 21 hook formulas

The first 5-30 seconds decide retention. Each formula: the pattern, when to use
it, and a fill-in template. Pick one per video; write 2-3 variants with it.
Rule for all 21: **the hook must confirm the title + thumbnail promise**, then
open a loop the video closes.

Add your own winners at the bottom with their retention numbers.

## Hook checklist
- First sentence ≤ 12 words. No "Hey guys, welcome back."
- Shows or says the thumbnail's promise within 5 seconds.
- Opens a loop ("...and the third one surprised me") the video closes later.
- Visual change in the first 2-3 seconds (cut, zoom, b-roll, text).
- Shorts: hook in 1-2 seconds, on-screen text too (many watch muted).
