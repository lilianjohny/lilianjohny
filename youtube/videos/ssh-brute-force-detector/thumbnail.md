# Thumbnail concepts

They support title #1 without repeating its words.

## A (recommended): the alert wall
- **Focal point:** a dark terminal filled with red `ALERT <ip>: <n> failed attempts`
  lines (real ones from your log, IPs partly blurred).
- **Face:** you on the right, looking at the screen, mildly alarmed, not cartoonish.
- **Text (3 words max):** `THEY'RE IN 24H?` or `EVERY. SINGLE. DAY.` (pick the one your data supports)
- **Colours:** near-black background, red alerts, one yellow text block for contrast.
- **The 1-second feeling:** "wait, is this happening to my server too?"

## B: the counter
- Huge number (your real failed-login count from 24h) in the middle, small text
  `failed logins` underneath, padlock icon cracking.
- No face. Works if you don't show your face.
- [CHECK] The number must be your real count.

## C: map of attackers
- World map with red dots for attacker IPs (look them up with any IP-geolocation
  tool), and a Python logo in the corner.
- Text: `ALL FROM ONE NIGHT`.

## Image-generator prompt (for a mock-up of A, not the final)
> YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, dark hacker-terminal screen filled with red alert lines
> of text, a person on the right side lit by the screen glow looking concerned,
> high contrast, bold yellow 3-word caption top-left, clean, no clutter

Final thumbnail: use a real screenshot of your terminal. It's more believable and
it's true.
